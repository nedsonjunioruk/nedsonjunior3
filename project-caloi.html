Caloi

Role: Script, Art Direction & Production

The Caloi project was an independent production, with script, characters and visual concept all developed from scratch as an independent piece. The intention was to tell a personal story and paint a portrait of Brazilian society, where the bicycle is more than a childhood toy. It is a landmark in the upbringing of an entire generation. Caloi was the natural choice: a Brazilian giant that has been part of every Brazilian's life at some point. Visuals and animations were generated with Nano Banana Pro and Kling 3.0, with post-production in Photoshop and Adobe Premiere.
