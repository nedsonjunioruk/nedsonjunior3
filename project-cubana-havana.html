Cubana × Havana Club

Role: Creative Direction, Script, AI Film Production & Editing

A cinematic manifesto for Cubana, the Cuban restaurant on Lower Marsh, Waterloo, marking the end of its partnership with Havana Club. Narrated by Bea Rangel, it is a thank-you letter to a summer of mojitos, music and dancing, and to the people who turned Cubana into casa.

Every scene starts from a real photograph or real footage of the venue and its people. Each frame was rebuilt with AI in a 35mm documentary look, keeping the real faces, places and brands intact, then animated using the original footage as the motion reference. The edit was finished with film grain, a location tag and bilingual subtitles.

From real photo to film

The film was built on what really happened at Cubana. Each shot began as a photo taken on the day, then became a cinematic frame with the same people and the same place.
