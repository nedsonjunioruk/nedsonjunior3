Red
Merlot

Role: Brand Identity & Art Direction

Red Merlot is an independent brand concept built around what wine truly offers beyond the glass: the warmth it awakens, the feeling of being alive and unique. The project centres on the relationship between a woman and the product, using that emotional charge as the foundation for the brand's identity and film narrative. Visuals and animations produced with Nano Banana Pro and Kling 3.0.
