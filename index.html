Creative Director  ·  Visual Artist

My relationship with art began before I could explain what art was. At 6 years old, I was already filling notebooks trying to recreate Manga characters I'd seen on TV. At 13, my first computer became a canvas, pixel by pixel, I built game characters in MS Paint. At 15, I discovered Photoshop, and that's when digital art and photography became something more than a hobby.

At 18, still images weren't enough. I wanted movement, rhythm, emotion. I started learning video editing and from that moment, I never looked back. I've worked with everything from outdated legacy software to the most advanced tools available today.

Now, at 30, living in London, I've added a new layer to my craft: AI. Not as a shortcut, but as an amplifier. With the right knowledge, AI doesn't replace creativity; it accelerates it, sharpens it, and makes consistent quality delivery possible at scale.

I'm a versatile creative who speaks fluently across different niches, because great storytelling transcends industry.
Art is my foundation. Everything else is the medium.
