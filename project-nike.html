Nike

Role: Advertising & Art Direction

An independent short film, written, directed and edited as a personal concept. The concept explores what makes the 2026 World Cup jersey distinctly Brazilian: the colours, the race, and the passion for football that runs through every Brazilian. Visual identity built from scratch using AI-generated imagery with Nano Banana Pro and Kling 3.0, cut in Adobe Premiere with compositing in Photoshop.
