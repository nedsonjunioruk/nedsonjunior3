Cubana

Role: Photography, Script & Video Editing

A brand film for Cubana, produced in partnership with Hennessy to promote Bad Bunny's London tour. The project started with on-location photography at Cubana's Lower Marsh Market venue, capturing the restaurant's Cuban-rooted energy, then grew into a full campaign shoot for the partnership. Every image — from the restaurant photography to the campaign visuals — was scripted and edited into a single brand film, bringing Cubana's atmosphere and Hennessy's premium positioning together for the tour's London activation.
